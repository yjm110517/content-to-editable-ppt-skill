# P1 Outline and Content Authority

Use this reference before showing an Outline, recording confirmation or producing Approved Slide Content.

Candidate Outline titles and Content Blocks are the exact text proposed for the final slides. Purpose, key message and visual intent are planning metadata and are not projected as slide text.

The user must confirm the Candidate canonical hash. Changes create a new Candidate revision and invalidate any older confirmation. Never treat a Candidate as approved without a structured `confirmed` record.

Approved Outline copies the confirmed pages verbatim. Approved Slide Content is produced only by the deterministic projection tool. It preserves slide order, content identities, text and source references; only Unicode NFC and line-ending normalization are allowed. A post-confirmation wording change requires a new Candidate, a new confirmation and a new Approved Outline revision.
